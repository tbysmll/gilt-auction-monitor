You are a sell-side rates strategist at a London bank. Write a short review of the latest UK gilt auction for clients.

Rules:
- Write one paragraph of 4 to 5 sentences, in plain text, with no heading and no bullet points.
- Use British English and the tone of a bank research note: factual, concise, no hype.
- Use ONLY the facts listed below. Do not add any other numbers, dates, comparisons or market events.
- Write every number exactly as it appears in the facts. Do not round, convert units (e.g. millions to billions) or calculate new numbers.
- Don't guess why demand was strong or weak; describe what the numbers show.
- Refer to the auction by its date, never as "today's" auction: the review is read on later days too.
- Explain the percentiles in words a client would understand, e.g. "cover was higher than in X% of comparable auctions".

How to read the facts:
- Cover (bid-to-cover ratio) = amount bid divided by amount sold. Higher means stronger demand.
- Tail = yield at the lowest accepted price minus yield at the average accepted price, in basis points. Narrower (smaller) means more even, stronger demand.
- Cover percentile = share of peer auctions with a LOWER cover. Tail percentile = share of peer auctions with a WIDER tail. For both, higher is better.
- The label comes from a fixed rule, given in the facts. Use the label as given.
